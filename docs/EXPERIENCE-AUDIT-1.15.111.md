# TGym 1.15.111 calendar and workout experience audit

Client date: **2026-11-03**. Published runtime: `d664d461bf5139d75991d7a1fe421bde77f1c78a`, Android versionCode 147. [Release](https://github.com/Truesilverking/TGym/releases/tag/v1.15.111).

## Result

The expanded Streak calendar shows the current streak on the left and the chosen local name on the right. Its profile photo stays in its original left-hand position beside the period selector, at least twice its previous size. The flame is larger than the consecutive-training-date number, and the redundant “Training days in this streak” caption is removed from this calendar header. The selected week, month or year and its distinct active-date count share one row. Narrow layouts give this photo/period row the available width, with period-navigation arrows beneath it; narrow month labels use the existing localized abbreviation while their accessible label retains the complete month. Existing counting, rest/pause continuity, date boundaries, colors, profile visibility and stored history retain their existing meanings.

All Streak report periods share the improved header and prominent local photo/name when profile visibility is enabled. Annual, Full and Complete-years PDF sections use three columns by four rows, in chronological month order, with one complete year per page. Week and month exports keep large calendar cells; multi-month/custom reports use readable grids and longer PDF ranges paginate without dropping or duplicating dates. PNG output retains the existing bounded two-times raster resolution; Streak PDFs embed lossless PNG images, including combined reports. Consistency calculations and its existing pagination remain independent. Actual downloaded week, month, annual, Full and Complete-years PDFs were inspected for page count, embedded-image dimensions and rendering, with hashes tied to this source's downloaded files.

Android workout notifications separate session status, set context and timer rows. The workout and rest chronometers retain their authoritative time bases, stable notification identity, accent validation, actions and background-service behavior. Long set labels are tested separately from clock rows, and the duration label is localized. Existing Web/PWA completion notices retain their browser/operating-system appearance; this change does not add an ongoing browser notification card or a custom iOS notification layout.

Session-only back-off weight overrides remain local to that session. Changing its Top Set now restores automatic weights for pending back-off rows, including rows whose weight was previously edited in that session. Regression coverage confirms that a new session uses the routine's automatic prescription again. Completed rows, manually recorded repetitions, explicit routine configuration, stable identities and historical data are preserved. The weight ruler now reaches adjacent whole-unit exercise loads directly even when the exercise loading increment is five or ten units. Untouched precise legacy values remain exact; canceling an editor discards its draft.
