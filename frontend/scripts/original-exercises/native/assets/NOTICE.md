The authoring JavaScript is TGym application code under AGPL-3.0-or-later.
The anatomical model and combined derived artwork have separate asset licenses.
The full anatomical attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 license remain in
[`../model/NOTICE.md`](../model/NOTICE.md) and the accompanying model files.

The unchanged Heatsy Body Model was adapted by DDDEV LTD from Z-Anatomy by
Gauthier Kervyn and Marcin Zielinski, derived from BodyParts3D, copyright The
Database Center for Life Science, original model by Kousaku Okubo. Its exact
56-bone rig, native hand/foot skin, muscle vocabulary and anatomical labels
remain the basis of this avatar. See the pinned source commit and hashes in
`source-provenance.json` and `../model/NOTICE.md`.

The continuous outer body starts with the MakeHuman core export distributed
in OpenGym3D's `walk.glb`. The publisher's `walk` manifest explicitly marks that
asset CC0-1.0; the relevant original entry is retained as
`opengym-walk-manifest.json`. Only its bind-rest surface is used. Its walking
motion, painted muscle colors, preview and third-party animation lanes are
not included in TGym artwork. MakeHuman core exported model data is CC0; this
does not relicense MakeHuman application code or unrelated third-party assets.

The replacement head/neck starts with MakeHuman's CC0 core `base.obj` at commit
`a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482`. The original upstream asset license
is retained byte-for-byte in `LICENSE-MAKEHUMAN-CC0.md`; `head-source.json`
records the source URL, commit, original OBJ hash and license hash. The baked
head geometry and its TGym runtime jaw/eye/paint refinements do not alter the
native rig or exercise mechanics. Full raw source meshes are available from
the recorded primary URLs and are not required by this baked-input renderer.

TGym changes include segment fitting and seam welding of the continuous body,
anatomy-based surface volume/chest adjustment, remapping skin weights to the
unchanged rig, transferring anatomical identity, fitting the head/neck, native
finger articulation, authored exercise poses/equipment/cameras/lights, and
rendering/encoding the final illustrations. `body-envelope.mesh.bin` and its
metadata retain the anatomical target's CC BY-SA 4.0 terms as combined
derivatives. Original CC0 sources remain CC0.

`anatomy-surface-input.png` is TGym's neutral four-view reference rendered from
the licensed anatomical model. `anatomy-surface-atlas.png` is an original static
painted anatomical texture generated with OpenAI imagegen from that reference.
The exact prompt and projection method are in `anatomy-surface-prompt.md`.
Both images are anatomical-model derivatives under CC BY-SA 4.0. One unchanged
four-view texture is projected in bind-rest coordinates and deformed by the
rig; exercise animation frames are not generated by imagegen.

The combined avatar and rendered anatomical GIF/PNG artwork retain CC BY-SA
4.0. This license does not change the application's AGPL source license or the
licenses of existing catalog artwork. No GymVisual input, logo, removed
watermark, commercial head or paid animation is included. Upstream creators
and licensors do not endorse TGym.

For the bent-knee calf illustration, the deep left soleus is represented by a
bounded surface highlight projected from its 244 real anatomical source
vertices. It is limited to the distal posterior and lateral calf borders,
uses a 30-to-55 mm projection falloff and preserves the original muscle IDs.
This illustration mask makes the deep target readable through the continuous
outer surface; it does not recolor the entire superficial gastrocnemius.

`source-provenance.json` describes the inputs and adaptations.
`pinned-inputs.json` records exact baked-input hashes checked by the renderer;
each export additionally records every shader, geometry, JSON, PNG, prompt and
notice hash and rejects source changes during the run.

This is an authoring candidate. Rendering and skeletal checks do not by
themselves approve visual style, skin collisions, physical device playback or
the user's exact exercise variant.
